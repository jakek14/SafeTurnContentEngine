Most MCA funders will accept less than what you owe. Almost no one initiates that conversation.

When a business can document three or more consecutive months of revenue decline, many funders will negotiate settlement rather than collect the full outstanding balance. In practice, 40–55 cents on the dollar is achievable for businesses that meet the conditions.

The conditions matter. The funder needs to believe that collecting the full amount is uncertain. That belief comes from documentation — bank statements, cash position, a clear picture of what's actually there.

This isn't forgiveness. It's a business calculation on their end.

Most owners assume the number on the agreement is fixed. The window to negotiate is before collections escalate — not after.
