Every MCA payment you make is invisible to the credit bureaus. The UCC filing isn't.

Most owners assume consistent payments build something — credit history, leverage for the next loan. They don't.

MCAs don't report payment history to any business credit bureau. Every payment leaves the account and builds nothing.

But when you took the advance, the funder filed a UCC-1. That lien does show up — on every business credit pull, it signals a security interest in your assets.

If the daily pull makes it hard to pay other bills — rent, utilities, vendors — those missed payments do report. The advance itself doesn't appear. The strain from carrying it does.

You pay on time. Nothing builds. The lien stays visible.
