Another loan won't fix what another loan created.

Most businesses in a cash flow crisis don't have a revenue problem. They have a debt structure problem.

The advances stacked. The daily payments compounded. And now every dollar that comes in is already spoken for before you can use it.

More funding just adds another layer to the same structure.

The answer isn't finding a lender who'll say yes. It's restructuring what you already owe.

That's where the breathing room actually comes from.
