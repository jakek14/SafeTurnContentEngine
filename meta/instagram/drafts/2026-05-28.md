Current on payments doesn't mean you're in control. It means you're in compliance.

The MCA structure is designed so the lender gets paid first. Before payroll. Before rent. Before anything you choose. It pulls automatically, daily, whether you had a strong week or a slow one.

When you stay current, the structure is working. The question is: for who?

Your goal and the lender's goal aren't the same. Their model works when you pay on time. Yours works when you build something.

If you're making every payment on time and still can't build reserves, can't hire, can't survive a slow month — staying current isn't a sign of strength. It's a sign of how hard you're working to serve a structure that isn't working for you.
