When the business can't pay, your name is next.

Most MCA agreements include a personal guarantee. Not as a footnote — as a primary enforcement mechanism.

If the business defaults, the funder doesn't stop at the business. They can pursue you personally.

That means your personal bank accounts. Your savings. Your home.

The LLC doesn't protect you here. The personal guarantee bypasses the business structure entirely.

Most owners signing in a cash emergency don't read past page three. The personal guarantee clause is usually on page four or five.

Find it before it finds you.
