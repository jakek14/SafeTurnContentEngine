Most MCA borrowers have options their lender hasn't mentioned. The window to use them closes the moment payments stop.

Before you miss a payment, you can ask for a temporary payment pause — a forbearance. You can request a reduced daily pull amount. You can ask for an extended repayment term, which spreads the same balance over more business days and lowers what leaves your account each morning.

None of these are guaranteed. But most MCA lenders would rather adjust terms than spend time and money on collections.

The conversation has to happen while you're still paying.

Knowing what to ask for changes what's possible.
