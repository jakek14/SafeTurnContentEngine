Two MCAs. You know each payment. You've never added them together.

Most owners with multiple advances know each individual pull. What most haven't done: write down every active advance, list the daily payment for each, and add them up.

Call it your blended daily obligation.

If your advances pull $490 and $730 from the same account each business day, you have $1,220 going out — every day, without exception. That's roughly $26,800 a month in MCA service alone.

Now add the rest. Rent, payroll, insurance, vendors — daily equivalents of every other fixed commitment. The actual cash leaving your account each day is probably a number you've never written down in one place.

The blended total matters because individual payments can feel manageable. The sum rarely does — and the sum is what your bank account has to keep pace with.

Write it down. One number. Today.
