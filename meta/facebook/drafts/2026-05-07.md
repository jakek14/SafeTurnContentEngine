Revenue tells you what came in. It doesn't tell you what's left.

Most owners track revenue. Some track profit. Neither tells you whether you can make payroll this Thursday.

The metric that actually matters: what's in your account after every scheduled payment clears.

Not what came in. Not what's on the P&L. What remains when the daily pulls, loan payments, and fixed obligations have moved through.

If that number is consistently thin — or negative — you have a structure problem. More revenue won't fix it. The obligation schedule doesn't respond to what this week looked like.

The businesses that get ahead of this start by measuring the right thing.
