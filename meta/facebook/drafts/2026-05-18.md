If your bank keeps saying no — your credit score probably isn't the problem.

When businesses carrying MCA debt hit a wall with traditional lenders, the instinct is to work on credit. Pay down a balance. Dispute an entry. Get the score moving.

That work matters for some financing. But it doesn't address what's actually failing the bank review.

Banks aren't declining based on your score. They're declining because your debt service coverage ratio falls below their threshold. Total income divided by total annual payment obligations — they need that number above 1.25x to approve.

Daily MCA pulls generate a debt service load that most bank underwriting can't absorb, regardless of your credit profile.

A 740 credit score doesn't move that calculation. Reducing what leaves the account every morning does.

The metric most owners are working on isn't the one that's blocking them.
