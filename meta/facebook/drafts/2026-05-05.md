39% of small business owners currently have less than one month of operating expenses in reserve. Not because they're not working hard. Not because their markets are dead. Because the structure of what they owe is consuming what they earn faster than they can replace it.

This is the difference between a revenue problem and a structure problem.

A revenue problem means you need more customers, better margins, higher prices.

A structure problem means the debt you're carrying is draining faster than you can refill.

Solving the first when you have the second is like pouring water into a bucket with a hole in the bottom.

The fix starts with looking at what you actually owe — to whom, at what cost, under what terms — and restructuring that debt so the business has room to breathe.

Not more sales. A better structure.
