The window to restructure MCA debt is widest when you're still making payments.

The moment payments stop, the nature of the conversation changes. NSF fees stack. Account monitoring shifts to collection protocols. Options that existed last week don't anymore.

This isn't about lender sympathy. It's about leverage.

A business that's still paying — even struggling — demonstrates operational capacity. That's what a lender needs to believe before agreeing to modified terms.

A business in full default has already answered the question they were hoping to negotiate around.

Restaurant and retail bankruptcy filings listing multiple MCAs as creditors rose again in Q1 2026. In many of those filings, businesses waited too long to have that conversation.

The best time to explore restructuring is before it feels urgent. That's also when you have the most options.
