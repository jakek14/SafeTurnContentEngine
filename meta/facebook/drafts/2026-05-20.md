Most MCA lenders don't have a workout department. They have a collections department.

When a bank loan gets into trouble, there's usually a relationship manager or workout team — someone whose job is to evaluate options and keep the loan performing.

MCA funders are structured differently. Most are built around speed of origination, not ongoing relationship management. When payments get strained, the operational response defaults to collection.

This isn't an accusation. It's how the product is designed.

The implication: if you're hoping to call your MCA lender and work something out — you may be calling the wrong party. The conversation most borrowers picture doesn't typically exist on that side of the table.

The discussions that actually create options — modified terms, reduced obligations, a real path forward — usually involve a different kind of expertise than the lender can offer.

Knowing that going in changes who you call first.
