Most business owners think an MCA is business debt. It is — until you default. Then the personal guarantee kicks in.

Almost every MCA agreement includes a personal guarantee. You, as an individual, agreed to repay the full balance if the business doesn't.

That means the funder can claim against your personal bank accounts, savings, and in some cases, home equity. Not just the business.

Unlimited personal guarantees — no cap on what can be pursued — are far more common in MCA contracts than limited ones.

MCA financing is positioned as capital without traditional collateral requirements. In many contracts, the personal guarantee replaced collateral with something broader.

Before signing any renewal or new advance, read the personal guarantee section carefully.
