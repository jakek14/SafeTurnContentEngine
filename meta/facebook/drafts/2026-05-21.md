Most business owners think restructuring means bankruptcy. It doesn't.

Restructuring, in most cases, is a private negotiation.

No courtrooms. No public filing. No trustee involved. Just a direct conversation — with the creditors you owe — working toward different terms.

The goal isn't to eliminate the debt. It's to change the structure of it: lower payment amounts, extended timelines, or a settlement at less than what you owe.

Bankruptcy requires a court, a trustee, and puts every obligation on the table — not just the ones creating pressure. It goes on the public record.

Most distressed SMBs don't need that. They need a credible path through the debt they're already carrying, negotiated privately, before the situation reaches a point that does require a court.

The fear of that label keeps a lot of owners from exploring options that are actually available right now.
