Most businesses in MCA debt are still generating revenue. The problem isn't the business.

A solvency problem: revenue can't cover costs regardless of how you structure things.

A cash flow structure problem: revenue comes in — and the obligation schedule takes it before you can deploy it. Same stress. Different cause. Different fix.

Most owners in MCA distress are dealing with the second problem, not the first.

A solvency crisis needs revenue growth, cost cuts, or an exit.

A structure crisis needs the structure fixed — daily obligations reduced, terms extended, debt consolidated. The business is still viable. It just needs room.

Confusing the two is how businesses that could survive end up making decisions designed for a crisis they're not actually in.

Before writing off the business — ask whether the business is actually the problem.

Usually, it isn't.
