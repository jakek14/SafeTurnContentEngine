Combined: $1,340 a day, every business day. Roughly $29,500 a month in MCA service alone.

The cash flow that felt manageable with one advance isn't there with two. Neither is any margin for a slow week.

The second advance didn't solve the cash flow problem. It doubled the daily obligation and started a new clock.

If you're considering another advance because the current one is making things hard — that's the signal. Not the solution.
